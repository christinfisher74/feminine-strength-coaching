Monday · Full Body Metabolic Circuit

3 rounds per circuit. Controlled pace, purposeful transitions, 45–75 seconds rest between rounds as needed.

Barbell Hip Thrust 3×12 · DB Squat to Press 3×10–12 · Bent-Over DB Row 3×12 · Reverse Lunge 3×8–10/side · Pallof Press 3×10/side.

DB Romanian Deadlift 3×12 · DB Chest Press 3×12 · Step-Ups 3×10/side · DB Lateral Raise 3×12 · Farmer Carry 3×40 ft.

Finisher: 2–3 rounds — KB swings ×15, lateral band walk ×10/side, squat hold ×30 sec.

Thursday · Full Body Conditioning Circuit

3 rounds per circuit. Trap-bar work is moderate and crisp, not a max-effort test.

Trap Bar Deadlift 3×10 · DB Walking Lunge 3×8/side · Lat Pulldown 3×12 · DB Shoulder Press 3×10–12 · Step-Down 3×8/side.

KB Goblet Squat 3×12–15 · Single-Arm Cable Row 3×10/side · DB Sumo Squat 3×12 · DB Curl to Press 3×10 · Glute Bridge March 3×10/side.

Finisher: 2–3 rounds — marching farmer carry ×30 sec, mountain climbers ×20 total, band abduction ×12/side.

Your coach will adjust exercises, loads and rounds to your current ability and recovery. Form comes before speed.
